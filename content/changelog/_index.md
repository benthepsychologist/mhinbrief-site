Every verified change to the registry lands here: field changes, new
records, retirements. Each entry carries the jurisdiction, what the value
was, what it is now, the source, and whether the change is enacted or in
force. The log is append-only — corrections are new entries, and nothing
is edited out of history. The weekly rollup is [the Bulletin](/newsletter/).
