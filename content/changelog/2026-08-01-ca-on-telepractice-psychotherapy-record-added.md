All professional standards apply equally to electronic, telephonic, in-person or hybrid practice. Obtain informed consent for the use of electronic media; take reasonable steps to ensure the technology is secure, confidential and appropriate to the client; confirm liability insurance covers electronic services BEFORE treating; comply with professional licensing requirements in the jurisdictions where clients are located; keep written/text/email correspondence in the clinical record; and do not rely on computer-generated assessments without exercising professional judgment. There is no requirement to live or work in Ontario to hold CRPO registration.

> "3.4.5 Registrants comply with relevant professional licensing requirements in the jurisdictions where clients are located." — CRPO, Professional Practice Standard 3.4 (Electronic Practice), published 2024-09-18, last modified 2026-01-07
