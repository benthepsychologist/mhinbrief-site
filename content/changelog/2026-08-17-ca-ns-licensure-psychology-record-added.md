Hold a licence from the Nova Scotia Regulator of Psychology (NSRP) to practise psychology. Practising licence categories are set by s. 8 (psychologist practising licence, plus any further category established in the bylaws); conditional licences by s. 9 (candidate psychologist conditional licence, psychologist conditional licence, plus bylaw categories). Registration criteria under s. 10(2)(a) require graduation from an approved master's, PhD or PsyD programme, passing the required examinations, English-language proficiency, Canadian citizenship or legal entitlement to work in Canada, and the capacity, competence and character to practise safely and ethically. Supervised-experience baseline per NSRP: two years post-doctoral, or four years at the master's level.

> "8 The categories of practising licence are (a) psychologist practising licence; (b) any other category of practising licence established in the bylaws." / "10(2)(a) ... capacity, competence and character to safely and ethically engage in the practice of the profession." — Psychology Regulations, N.S. Reg. 252/2025 (O.I.C. 2025-337), in force 2025-11-24
