The weekly Bulletin **is** the changelog: every verified change to the
registry — what changed, in which jurisdiction, since when, with the
source attached — rolled up once a week. No commentary padding, no
introduced-bill noise, no "5 things every therapist must know."

If nothing verified changed, the Bulletin says so. That is also
information.

**Signup opens with Issue #1.** The registry is being built and sourced
now; the first issue ships when the first entries do. Until then, the
[changelog](/changelog/) is public and this page is the only announcement
channel.
