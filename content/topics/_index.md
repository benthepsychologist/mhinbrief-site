Where a single obligation varies across all thirteen jurisdictions, a
matrix beats thirteen hub pages. Planned first set, in build order:

- **[Telepractice & cross-border work](/topics/telepractice/)** — which
  regulator governs when the client and the clinician sit in different
  places, and what your college says about practising from abroad.
- **[GST/HST](/topics/tax/)** — the 2024 exemption for psychotherapy and
  counselling therapy services, and the traps that came with it.
- **[Privacy & data residency](/topics/privacy/)** — which statute governs
  a private practice in each province, and what crossing a provincial
  border does.
- **[Records retention](/topics/retention/)** — minimum periods,
  minor-client rules, and where no standard is published.
- **[Liability insurance](/topics/insurance/)** — required coverage and,
  critically, coverage territory.
- **[Licensure & registration](/topics/licensure/)** — registration
  classes, the degree and supervised-hour requirements behind each, and
  where the same title means different things in different provinces.
- **AI in practice** — obligations that attach to the clinician using
  AI-enabled tools. One topic among the others, deliberately not the
  front door.

Matrices appear as their cells clear verification; a cell without a source
and a last-verified date does not ship.
