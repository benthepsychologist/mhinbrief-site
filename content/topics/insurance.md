Two separate questions live here, and the second is the one that catches
people. First: does your regulator require professional liability insurance
at all, and at what limits? That answer is not uniform, even between
colleges in the same province.

Second, and less documented anywhere: **where does your coverage actually
apply?** Regulator by-laws are frequently silent on territory, while the
insurance programs practitioners actually buy differ sharply — some follow
you worldwide, some stop at the Canadian border, and some address travelling
with your existing clients without ever addressing clients based elsewhere.
The matrix separates what the regulator requires from what a policy is
documented to cover, because conflating them is how a gap goes unnoticed.
