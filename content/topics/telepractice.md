The general principle is easy to state and easy to get wrong: it is usually
the **client's** location that governs, not the practitioner's. The hard
part is that regulators do not agree on what follows from it. Two colleges
in the same province can take materially different positions on whether an
out-of-province practitioner needs to register before seeing a client here.

The matrix below records each regulator's actual position, quoted verbatim,
along with whether it sits in an enforceable standard or in guidance — a
distinction worth keeping, because "the college's website says" and "the
college's standard requires" are not the same obligation.
