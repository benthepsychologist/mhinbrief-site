Two questions decide a practitioner's privacy obligations, and neither is
usually the one people ask. First: which statute actually governs you —
which turns on how the legislation captures your profession, and that route
is not the same for every discipline even inside one province. Second: what
happens when client information leaves the province, whether through a
hosted platform, a backup, or a colleague across a border.
