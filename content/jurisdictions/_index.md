title: "Jurisdictions"
description: "Province-by-province hubs for the rules governing behavioural-health practice."
---

Regulation of behavioural-health practice in Canada is provincial: colleges
rather than boards, controlled acts and protected titles that differ
sharply at each border, and no federal license. Each hub collects, for one
jurisdiction: the regulators, title protection and controlled acts,
telepractice and cross-border rules, privacy and records obligations,
insurance requirements, and tax treatment — each claim sourced and dated.

**Build order:** Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Alberta, and
Nova Scotia first; the remaining provinces as single pages; the
territories as short entries. United States coverage follows, opening with
the interstate compacts. Hubs appear here as their entries clear
verification — nothing ships before its receipts do.
